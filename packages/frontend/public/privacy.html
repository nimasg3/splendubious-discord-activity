Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

1. Overview

Splendubious ("the Application") is a Discord Activity. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

When you use the Application inside Discord, we receive the following information from Discord's OAuth2 flow:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • Your Discord username and display name
  • Your Discord avatar hash (used to display your profile picture in-game)
  • The Discord guild (server) and channel you are in

We do not collect email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any other personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data above is used solely to:

  • Identify players within a game session
  • Display your name and avatar to other players in the same game
  • Associate you with the correct game room for your Discord voice channel

4. Data Retention

Game session data (including player names and IDs) is stored in memory on our server only for the duration of the game session. It is not written to a persistent database and is discarded when the session ends or the server restarts.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Your information is only visible to other players in the same game session.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application runs within Discord. Discord's own Privacy Policy governs the data Discord collects about you independently.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect information from children under the age required by Discord's Terms of Service. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
